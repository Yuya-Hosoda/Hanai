## 1. Original Research Theme

> A method for action planning in mental health care support dialogue robots through the integration of asynchronous knowledge retrieval and action generation based on MoshiRAG.

**Original research questions (user-provided):**
- RQ1: Can clinically grounded voice responses be generated by integrating a mental health-specific external knowledge base into MoshiRAG's asynchronous RAG mechanism?
- RQ2: Can an action planning module generate predefined gesture commands asynchronously and safely, based on dialogue phase and user emotional state?
- RQ3: Does the integrated output of voice responses and gesture actions contribute to improving the user's subjective sense of security, trust, and information comprehension?

## 4. Main Contribution Direction

**Three specific contributions (from `contribution_statement.md`):**

**Contribution 1 — Async RAG for full-duplex spoken dialogue (RQ1)**
First peer-reviewed engineering specification of an asynchronous RAG integration within a full-duplex voice dialogue system for a mental health-specific knowledge base. Exploits the temporal gap between voice response onset and informational content delivery. Novelty established by MG-02 (async RAG for FD voice not yet a peer-reviewed category).

**Contribution 2 — BT gesture planner with full-duplex timing model (RQ2)**
A Behavior Tree-based predefined gesture command planner operating concurrently with full-duplex voice generation, plus the first engineering-derived timing model for gesture dispatch under full-duplex latency constraints. Novelty established by MG-03 (no async gesture dispatch system), MG-04 (no unified system), and MG-05 (no FD timing model).

**Contribution 3 — Tier 4 gesture safety constraints (RQ2 + Safety)**
First principled definition of gesture-level safety constraints (GB-1: predefined vocabulary only; GB-2: state-conditional suppression; GB-3: emergency stop) for mental health support robot interaction. Adapted from text-chatbot safety frameworks to voice/embodiment contexts. Novelty established by MG-08 (no voice-robot safety framework) and MG-10 (no gesture safety standard).

**What is integration only (not novel):**
Full-duplex LLM foundation (E1–E5), RAG formulation (E8, E10, E11), BT formalism (E13), BEAT gesture vocabulary (E19), safety principles (S2–S7), half-duplex voice+gesture coordination (E20).

## 6. Key Findings from Each Unit

### U1 — Full-Duplex Spoken LLMs
- Full-duplex spoken dialogue modeling without text supervision is demonstrated [dGSLM, TACL 2023]
- Interactive response latency (~200ms) is a design target reported by Moshi [arXiv — reference only]
- Token-level fusion for simultaneous listening and speaking is feasible [LSLM, AAAI 2025]
- Neural FSM approach achieves <500ms latency in >50% of interactions [NeurIPS 2024]
- A taxonomy of FD-SLM architectures (Engineered vs Learned Synchronization) exists [arXiv survey — emerging]
- **Gap:** All full-duplex systems lack domain-specific knowledge grounding

### U2 — RAG Architectures
- Lewis et al. (NeurIPS 2020) establishes the canonical parametric + non-parametric RAG framework
- FLARE (EMNLP 2023) and Self-RAG (ICLR 2024) establish when-to-retrieve and whether-to-retrieve mechanisms
- WavRAG (ACL 2025) demonstrates audio-native RAG at 10× acceleration over ASR-mediated pipelines
- Stream RAG (arXiv, under review) demonstrates streaming parallel query prediction for spoken dialogue
- **Gap:** Async RAG for full-duplex spoken dialogue is not yet a peer-reviewed architectural category [MG-02]

### U7 — Social Robot Action Planning and Gesture Generation
- Behavior Trees provide modular, reactive, formally analyzable robot behavior architecture [CRC Press 2018]
- BTs outperform FSMs empirically as behavioral complexity increases [IEEE TSE 2023]
- Parallel simultaneous text+gesture generation is feasible with negligible overhead on Pepper/Furhat [Frontiers 2025]
- BT-based action planning is deployed in care-adjacent social robot contexts [HRI 2022 — elderly support]
- **Gap:** No peer-reviewed system addresses async predefined gesture dispatch under full-duplex voice [MG-03/04]

### U8 — Voice-Gesture Synchronization
- BEAT taxonomy provides semantic gesture vocabulary (beat/iconic/deictic/metaphoric) with onset-stroke-retraction structure [ECCV 2022] — vocabulary reference only; human timing data ≠ robot dispatch timing
- Voice+gesture coordination in real dialogue robots is demonstrated in half-duplex systems [Frontiers 2022]
- Backchannel timing and gesture dispatch timing are distinct problems [INTERSPEECH 2025; governance: MG-06]
- Real-time multimodal LM with structured tool dispatch provides architectural precedent [arXiv 2026 — preprint]
- **Gap:** No peer-reviewed timing model specifies gesture dispatch under full-duplex constraints [MG-05]; available evidence base is predominantly half-duplex [MG-07]

### U9 — HRI Measurement Instruments
- Godspeed Questionnaire Series [IJSR 2009]: most cited HRI instrument; provides Perceived Safety subscale (maps to "perceived security" construct in RQ3) — user perception only
- Hancock et al. 2021 [Human Factors]: updated trust meta-analysis; robot performance is the strongest trust factor
- Rubagotti et al. 2022 [RAS]: six factors of perceived safety including control and transparency
- JMIR 2024 systematic review: 27 validated HRI instruments; identifies gap in clinical-context instruments
- Almere Model [IJSR 2010]: validated acceptance scale for assistive social robots (elderly context)
- Robot Social Presence Scale [Sci. Reports 2023]: 5-dimensional, 17-item scale; Dimension 3 provides perceived information understanding
- **Gap:** No validated HRI information comprehension scale exists — custom test required [MG-U9-01]; context-transfer to mental health robot context requires pilot [MG-U9-02]; "perceived security" construct requires composite operationalization [MG-U9-03]

### U10 — Safety and Ethics for Mental Health AI
- WHO 2024 identifies accountability gaps, automation bias, and absence of human oversight as key LMM risks in health
- JMIR 2024 identifies absence of duty of care and emotional manipulation risk as specific mental health AI concerns
- Stepped-care model [Frontiers 2026] proposes AI as low-intensity tier with mandatory professional escalation — perinatal context; generalization requires qualification
- Current chatbot systems fail minimum crisis safety criteria [Scientific Reports 2025]
- Human-in-the-loop is an active design requirement, not a fallback [Nature Medicine 2025]
- Mental health interaction data = GDPR Art. 9 sensitive category / HIPAA PHI [PMC 2025]
- **Gap:** No safety framework addresses voice-based embodied robots [MG-08]; no gesture safety standard for mental health robots [MG-10]; Japanese regulatory context not covered [MG-09]
