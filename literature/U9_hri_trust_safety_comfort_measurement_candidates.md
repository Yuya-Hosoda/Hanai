## 3. Measurement Construct Mapping

The following table maps each target RQ3 construct to candidate instruments. Governance constraint: all constructs are user-perception measures only; none constitute clinical assessments.

| Construct | Definition (for this study) | Candidate instrument(s) | Governance note |
|-----------|----------------------------|------------------------|-----------------|
| **Trust** | User's subjective confidence in the robot's reliability and intent | C03 (Hancock 2021 trust model) + validated scale identified via C05 | Trust in robot = user perception — NOT clinical therapeutic alliance |
| **Perceived safety** (physical/threat) | User's perception of the robot as non-threatening and predictable in physical proximity | C01 (Godspeed Perceived Safety subscale), C04, C09 | Physical safety perception — NOT clinical safety assessment or system safety validation |
| **Perceived security** (psychological) | User's sense of psychological comfort and safety in disclosing to the robot | C01 (Perceived Safety items adapted), C06 (Anxiety subscale, inverted), C04 (control + transparency factors) | Psychological security = subjective self-report — NOT clinical anxiety reduction |
| **Comfort** | User's subjective ease during the interaction | C04 (comfort as a key factor), C06 (anxiety items inverted) | Comfort = user self-report — NOT therapeutic outcome |
| **Naturalness** | User's perception of the robot's interaction as fluid and human-like | C01 (Animacy + Likeability subscales), C07 (social presence scale) | Naturalness = perception — NOT evidence of clinical rapport or therapeutic alliance |
| **Social presence** | User's sense that the robot is a social being during interaction | C07 (5-dimensional, 17-item scale), C01 (Anthropomorphism subscale) | Social presence = user perception — NOT evidence of human-equivalent care |
| **User acceptance** | User's intention to use and attitude toward the robot | C06 (Almere model full scale: trust, social presence, usefulness, anxiety, intent) | Acceptance = behavioral intention — NOT clinical recommendation or endorsement |
| **Information comprehension** | User's accurate recall and understanding of information delivered by the robot | **[MATERIAL GAP MG-U9-01]** — no validated HRI instrument found | Comprehension = recall accuracy — NOT treatment adherence or clinical information uptake |
| **Cognitive load** | User's subjective mental effort during the interaction | Not in approved corpus — NASA-TLX exists but was not returned in approved search results | If used: subjective workload = user perception — NOT clinical cognitive assessment |
| **Clinical safety** | Whether the system is safe for clinical use | BLOCKED — outside RQ3 scope | Must not be measured or claimed |
| **Clinical efficacy** | Whether the system provides therapeutic benefit | BLOCKED — outside RQ3 scope | Must not be measured or claimed |

## 4. Three-Group Classification

### Group 1 — Adopt (6 papers)

| # | Title | Rationale |
|---|-------|-----------|
| C01 | Godspeed Questionnaire (IJSR 2009) | Most widely cited HRI measurement instrument per Scopus; provides validated Perceived Safety subscale (maps to "perceived security") and Animacy/Likeability subscales (maps to "perceived naturalness"); semantic differential format compatible with short interaction studies |
| C03 | Hancock et al. 2021 evolving trust meta-analysis | Most current meta-analytic evidence base for trust-in-robot measurement; updated three-category model (human / robot / context factors); supersedes C02 for instrument selection; provides theoretical framing for trust operationalization |
| C04 | Rubagotti et al. 2022 perceived safety survey | Only comprehensive survey of perceived safety assessment methods in HRI; identifies six key factors (context, comfort, experience/familiarity, trust, sense of control, transparency/predictability); directly relevant to "perceived security" and "perceived comfort" constructs |
| C05 | JMIR 2024 systematic review | Reviews 27 validated HRI psychological instruments with psychometric properties across 34 studies; most complete evidence base for instrument selection; notes that no instrument with measurement error and responsiveness metrics exists for healthcare robot contexts — critical limitation awareness |
| C06 | Heerink et al. Almere Model (IJSR 2010) | Validated acceptance scale for assistive social robots in care-adjacent context (elderly care); includes trust, social presence, perceived usefulness, anxiety, and adaptivity constructs; most relevant user acceptance model for a care-support robot interaction |
| C07 | Robot Social Presence Scale (Sci. Reports 2023) | Validated 5-dimensional, 17-item scale for robot social presence; maps to "perceived naturalness" and "social presence" constructs; developed and validated with peer review |

| C08 | Self-Disclosure to Robots / Emotional Distress (IJSR 2024) | Contains user perception data from a care-adjacent emotional disclosure context; hold pending full content verification to confirm it reports only perception measures; **governance constraint**: if any clinical outcome claims appear in the paper, those specific claims are blocked from use |

| MG-U9-01 | **No validated HRI instrument found for information comprehension in voice-and-gesture robot dialogue contexts.** NASA-TLX (cognitive workload) exists but is not in the approved corpus and measures workload rather than comprehension. | HIGH | Define a custom 3–5 item recall/application comprehension test based on specific informational content in the interaction scenario (e.g., "What did the robot suggest you do when feeling overwhelmed?"). ## 7. Recommended RQ3 Evaluation Instrument Set

Based on the U9 Adopt group. All items require L3 verification and pilot testing (MG-U9-02) before deployment in the main user study.

```
CONSTRUCT 1 — Perceived security / psychological safety
  Primary:      Godspeed Perceived Safety subscale [C01]
                → 2–5 semantic differential items (e.g., anxious/relaxed, agitated/calm)
  Supplementary: Almere Model "Anxiety" items, inverted [C06]
  Supplementary: Items derived from Rubagotti taxonomy:
                "sense of control over interaction" + "transparency/predictability" factors [C04]
  Governance:   Label as USER PERCEPTION of safety — NOT clinical safety assessment
                — NOT evidence that the system is clinically safe

CONSTRUCT 2 — Trust in robot
  Primary:      Validated trust scale identified via JMIR 2024 systematic review [C05]
                → Select instrument with established structural validity and internal
                  consistency (criteria: included in the 27 instruments surveyed by C05)
  Framework:    Hancock 2021 three-category model (human / robot / context) [C03]
                for interpreting trust measurement results
  Governance:   Trust = user perception of robot reliability — NOT therapeutic alliance
                — NOT clinical trustworthiness of the system

CONSTRUCT 3 — Perceived naturalness / interaction quality
  Primary:      Robot Social Presence Scale (5-dimensional, 17 items) [C07]
                → Covers interactive expression, information understanding,
                  emotional interdependence, and interaction behaviour perception
  Supplementary: Godspeed Animacy and Likeability subscales [C01]
  Governance:   Naturalness = user perception — NOT evidence of clinical rapport
                — NOT evidence that gesture improves mental health outcomes

CONSTRUCT 4 — Information comprehension
  Primary:      [MATERIAL GAP MG-U9-01] — no validated HRI instrument available
  Recommended:  Custom 3–5 item recall/application test
                → Items are scenario-specific (based on content delivered by robot
                  during the experimental interaction scenario)
                → Examples: "What did the robot recommend you do when feeling
                  overwhelmed?" (recall); "If you felt distressed, what would be
                  the first step the robot suggested?" (application)
                → Validate via expert review before use; report item-level agreement
  Governance:   Comprehension = accuracy of information recall — NOT treatment
                adherence — NOT clinical knowledge uptake — NOT health literacy

CONSTRUCT 5 — User acceptance (optional, supporting measure)
  Primary:      Almere Model short form [C06]
                → Subscales: trust, social presence, perceived usefulness, anxiety,
                  intention to use
                → Note: developed for elderly users; pilot-test for population
                  transfer before using in main study
  Governance:   Acceptance = behavioral intention to use — NOT clinical recommendation
                — NOT evidence that the system should be deployed clinically

COMPARISON CONDITION DESIGN
  Minimum: voice-only condition vs. voice+gesture condition (within-subjects or
           between-subjects depending on feasibility)
  Rationale: RQ3 asks whether integrated output (voice+gesture) improves perception
             compared to a baseline; no condition comparison → no RQ3 answer
  Governance: Results describe perception differences across conditions —
              they do NOT constitute evidence that gesture produces clinical benefit
```
